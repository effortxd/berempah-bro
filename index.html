The Story

MasterChef Fame,
Hawker Game

Derek Cheong won MasterChef Singapore Season 2 — then traded the studio kitchen for a hawker stall at Beauty World Food Centre, chasing one obsession: pushing ayam goreng berempah further than anyone thought a hawker plate could go.

Every cut is massaged with a rempah of sixteen spices — turmeric, cumin, coriander and thirteen friends — and left to marinate overnight. Fried to order. Then the signature move: a shower of fried garlic, breadcrumbs, kerisik and Japanese sakura ebi. Prawn cereal meets typhoon-shelter crab, on coconut rice.

One stall became six, across two brands — Berempah Bros and sister stall Gepuk Guys. Same fire, same crunch, every single plate.

"One dish, done properly, with a twist you won't find anywhere else."

Derek Cheong · @the.rek · Founder

Good Questions

Before You Ask, Bro

What exactly is "berempah"? +

"Berempah" is Malay for "spiced". Traditional ayam goreng berempah is fried chicken marinated in a rempah — a fresh-ground spice paste. Ours uses sixteen spices, rests overnight, and gets finished with a crunch topping you won't find at any other stall.

Is Berempah Bros halal? +

We're not halal-certified — the Berempah Bros menu includes a pork dish, and proteins share the same fryers. Sister stall Gepuk Guys (Bedok) uses no pork and no lard, though it isn't certified either. We get asked this a lot and we'd rather be upfront about it.

Who's behind this? +

Derek Cheong (@the.rek) — an engineering undergrad who became MasterChef Singapore's youngest champion at 23 (Season 2, 2021). He took the trophy into hawker centres on purpose, building Berempah Bros and sister brand Gepuk Guys into six stalls across the island.

Do you deliver? +

Yes — islandwide via Oddle, and foodpanda from selected outlets. Or drop by any of our six stalls; food is always freshest straight from the fryer.

Which cut should I get? +

Thigh if you want it juicy, breast if you like it lean — those are the two cuts on the menu. There is no wrong answer — the 16-spice rempah does the heavy lifting either way.
